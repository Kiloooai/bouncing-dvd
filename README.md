# 📀 Bouncing DVD

*The classic screensaver, upgraded. Bounce the DVD logo, hit corners, don't fail. It gets faster. It gets sassy.*

---

## What is this?

Remember that old DVD player screensaver where the logo bounces around? This is that, but with personality. The logo changes color every time it hits a wall. Hitting a corner gives you a point and speeds up the game. Miss the corner? You'll get a sarcastic remark. Survive as long as you can — the pace increases over time.

It's a test of timing, luck, and maybe your life choices.

---

## Features

- **Classic bouncing mechanics** — DVD logo ricochets off screen edges
- **Color cycling** — new random neon color on every wall hit
- **Corner scoring** — landing exactly on a corner awards a point and increases speed
- **Speed escalation** — the game gets progressively faster
- **Streak counter** — track consecutive corner hits
- **Sarcastic commentary** — roasts you on missed corners, celebrates successes
- **Visual effects** — pulsating messages, screen crack effect on big misses
- **Click boost** — click anywhere to temporarily increase speed
- **Right-click reset** — start over when you inevitably fail
- **Single HTML file** — instant nostalgia

---

## How to Play

1. Open `index.html`
2. Watch the DVD logo bounce
3. No, you don't control it directly — it just bounces
4. **Your goal:** hope it hits the corners
5. That's it. It's a screensaver. But with scores.

*Advanced strategy:* The speed increases each time a corner is hit, making subsequent corners harder. Try to survive as long as possible without missing corners. Click to give it a temporary speed burst (risky). Right-click to reset everything.

---

## The Real Story

It's a screensaver. But it's also a metaphor: things bounce around until they eventually break. Also, I wanted to see how many people would actually try to "play" a screensaver. (You are.)

The sarcastic messages are optional — they appear occasionally to remind you that, statistically, you're not good at predicting bounces.
